About the Jazz Orchestra

Ashland University’s Jazz Orchestra is one of the finest in Ohio. Made up of both majors and non-majors, the Orchestra performs for concerts and campus events throughout the year. In addition, the Jazz Orchestra hosts the annual Maplerock Jazz Festival, inviting outstanding high school jazz ensembles to perform and work with the finest professional jazz musicians in the country. This ensemble tours annually and an audition is required. Small jazz combos are also formed as schedules allow.

Program Highlights

  • Annual tours around the country
  • Multiple concerts per semester featuring soloists and guest artists
  • Recording opportunities for portfolio development
  • Jazz festivals and competitions regionally and nationally
  • Open to all majors — no audition required for enrollment, but participation in performances requires successful audition
