Ashland Area Community Concert Band

This ensemble is designed for both university students and members of the Ashland community. The ensemble rehearses one evening each week and performs a wide variety of literature from Broadway selections and classic marches to masterworks transcribed or written for concert band. No audition is required. The band currently rehearses on Tuesday nights from 7:30 to 9:00 pm.
