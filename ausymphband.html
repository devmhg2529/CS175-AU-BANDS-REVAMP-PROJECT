Audition Information

Interested in joining the Symphonic Band?

The Ashland University Symphonic Band requires auditions for admittance into the ensemble. These auditions will take place in November of the Fall semsester, and January of the Spring semester. Enrollment in the course (MUSIC*010B/010C) is required to be notified of audition dates & times.
Auditions will begin on November 2nd, 2026 at 4:30pm and will take place during selected audition time slots. The linked audition packets below will be the music pieces that will be used for the audition process for each instrument.
For more information, please contact Director of Bands, Professor Slade Presley Denman at sdenman@ashland.edu
